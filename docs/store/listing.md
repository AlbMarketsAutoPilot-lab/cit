## Full description

```
Think you know football? Prove it to your friends.

Called It! is a free football prediction game for groups of friends. Pick the result of today's and upcoming matches, call the exact score if you dare, and climb your group's table.

HOW IT WORKS
• Pick 1, X or 2 – or play it safer with a double: 1X, X2 or 12.
• Every pick shows its points before you choose. Underdogs are worth more, safe picks less.
• Call the exact score for a +3 bonus.
• Change your picks until kickoff. Then they lock.
• Points come in automatically when the match ends (regular time).

PLAY WITH YOUR FRIENDS
• Create a group and invite friends with a code or a link.
• Each group follows one competition, with tables for this round, this week and this month.
• After kickoff, see how everyone in your group picked.
• When the season ends (or the admin ends the round), the top three take the podium – then start a new round.

TRACK YOUR FORM
• Your hit rate, results, best pick types and points per month.
• Streaks of right picks in a row and points per competition.

MATCHES
Top European leagues, the European club cup, the Brazilian league and the big international tournaments. Team crests, league positions and points per game help you decide.

SIMPLE AND PRIVATE
• No email, no phone number, no social login: just pick a nickname.
• A recovery code lets you move to a new phone.
• Optional daily match reminder.
• Delete your account and all your data at any time.

JUST FOR FUN
Called It! is a game. There is no money, no betting, no prizes and no odds. Points are only for bragging rights.

Match data by football-data.org.
```

## App access instructions

```
No username or password is needed. Anyone can create an account in the app:
1. Open the app and type any new nickname (3–16 letters, numbers or _), for example a random one like Review2026x.
2. Tap "Start playing".
3. A popup shows a recovery code. Tap "OK".
All features are then available. To test groups, tap Groups and create a group (no invite code needed).
There is no email, phone number, payment or third-party sign-in.
```
