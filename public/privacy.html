Privacy policy

Called It! is a free football prediction game. Last updated: 25 October 2026.

What we store

The nickname you choose, a random account number, your match predictions, your points and the groups you join. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.

Your sign-in key and recovery code are stored on our server only as one-way hashes.

Who can see it

Members of your groups see your nickname, your points and, once a match has started, your pick for that match.

Services we use

Our server runs on Cloudflare. Match data comes from football-data.org (it receives no data about you).

The Android app uses Google AdMob to show ads, which may use your device's advertising ID, and OneSignal to send match reminders, which uses a push token for your device. Their privacy policies: Google, OneSignal. In the European Economic Area and the UK you are asked for consent before personalised ads, and you can change it any time in Me → Ad privacy settings.

How long we keep it

Predictions are kept for up to 60 days and daily point totals for up to 400 days. Accounts not used for 12 months are deleted automatically.

Deleting your account

In the app or on the web version: Me → Delete account. This removes your nickname, predictions, points and memberships immediately. If you lost your phone, see delete your account.

Children

Called It! is not directed at children under 13.
